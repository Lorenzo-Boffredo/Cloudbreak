They appear in the bottom-right corner, above the clock, and stay in the notification center. The Cloudbreak icon sits in the tray next to the clock.

App icon

The same ring as the tray icon, on a dark tile so it reads on light and dark Windows. Used for the Start menu, the taskbar, the installer and the desktop shortcut. The small logo inside the app is the same mark without the tile.

Tray icon

The ring is one round: the long arc is the hold, the short one is the break. It fills as time passes and updates once a minute, so it costs nothing.

Tray menu

Right-click the tray icon. Mini timer shows or hides the chip, same as the switch in Settings.

Colors

Break fog

When a break starts, a little violet mist appears in the corners of every screen you have connected. It stays about 10 seconds, then fades away. Clicks pass straight through it.

Break fog is off in Settings.
